# Retail Sales Analysis Report

## Project Title
Real-world Data Project (Retail Sales)

## Dataset
Retail sales data containing products and sales values.

## Analysis
- Calculated statistical summaries.
- Identified best-selling products.
- Visualized sales distribution.

## Key Findings
- Laptop is the top-performing product.
- Phones are the second highest-selling category.
- Retail sales vary significantly between products.

## Conclusion
Data analysis helps businesses identify high-performing products and make informed decisions.
